What we build

A working workshop that travels with the crew.

Al Furat designs and fabricates customized trucks and mobile workshop vehicle conversions in the UAE for industrial, construction, maintenance and remote-site requirements. Each build takes a commercial chassis or van body and turns it into a self-contained working space: a lined cabin with an integrated workstation, shelving, a workbench and a shadow board, so tools stay organised and within reach. These industrial service vehicles suit teams working away from a fixed base — maintenance crews, field-service engineers and contractors — where the cost is time lost travelling back for a tool, a part or a bench. Layout follows the work the vehicle has to do, then the fit-out is fabricated to survive the environment it will do it in.

  • Configured around a specific operational brief, not a fixed catalogue layout
  • Internal storage and workstation, plus external accessories such as roof racks and toolboxes
  • Rugged construction intended for demanding industrial environments
Design

Designed around the job, not the vehicle.

Every build starts with the work. We look at what the crew carries, what they use most often, and what has to be reached first — then plan the internal layout around that sequence rather than filling the body with generic racking.

Frequently used equipment sits within reach of the door. Heavier items are mounted low and secured. Panels, benches and mounts are fabricated to hold their alignment while the vehicle is working, because a fit-out that loosens on rough ground stops being useful quickly.

  • Customer-specific requirements captured before layout begins
  • Internal layout planned around access and frequency of use
  • Durable fabrication and secure equipment mounting
  • Organised storage that keeps the crew productive on site

Capability

Take the workshop to the work.

These vehicles are built for rugged industrial environments and for reaching sites that a standard service vehicle struggles to serve. Specified with off-road capability, they are intended to travel rocky trails, muddy tracks and steep inclines to reach remote construction and service locations.

The point is straightforward: the tools, the bench and the working space arrive with the crew, so work can begin on arrival rather than after a return trip.

  • Built for demanding industrial and construction environments
  • Specified for access to remote and difficult sites
  • Rocky, muddy and steep terrain considered in the specification
  • Tools and workspace delivered directly to the jobsite

Questions

Common questions.

What can be customised on an industrial vehicle?

In a mobile workshop conversion, the internal layout and the external accessories. Inside, the workshop cabin can be configured with storage and an integrated workstation. Outside, accessories such as roof racks and toolboxes can be added. What suits your vehicle depends on the body and the work it has to do.

Can the vehicle include a mobile workstation?

Yes. An integrated workstation combining a workbench, shelving and a shadow board is a standard part of the fit-outs we build, positioned so the crew can work inside the body.

What storage options can be integrated?

Shelving, drawer units, cabinets and a shadow board internally, with externally accessed toolboxes where items are better kept outside the cabin. Sizes and positions are set by what you carry.

Can these vehicles be designed for remote worksites?

Yes. They are built for rugged industrial environments and can be specified for off-road use, so they can reach remote construction and service locations over rocky, muddy or steep ground.

Can roof racks, toolboxes and equipment mounts be included?

Yes. Roof racks and external toolboxes are common additions, and equipment carried on board can be securely mounted as part of the build.

What information is needed to start an enquiry?

The intended application, the vehicle or chassis you have in mind if any, the equipment and storage you need, the operating environment and the project location. That is enough for a first conversation.
