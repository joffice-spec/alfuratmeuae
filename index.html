INTRODUCING AQUILA

Aquila means eagle in Latin — strength, precision and reliability.

Al Furat Group has manufactured vehicle bodies in the UAE since 1988. Aquila is its recovery vehicle line: light, medium and heavy duty car carriers engineered to the same standard the eagle is named for — sharp vision, exact execution, no compromise on the day the work is hardest.

ENGINEERING & RECOVERY TECHNOLOGY

Engineered for recovery. Not adapted for it.

Every Aquila carrier is built around six systems — structure, lift, pull, hydraulics, sight and storage. The same engineering standard applies from the 400 to the 1200.

12,000 → 30,000 LB

Structural deck

Full steel construction, built to be tough and reliable. Steel or aluminium decks on medium duty, with multiple length and width configurations.

UP TO 20,000 LB

Winch systems

Up to 12,000 lb capacity winches for maximum pulling power on light and medium duty. Heavy duty runs a 20,000 lb worm gear winch for dependability and precision control.

3,500 / 5,000 / 7,500 LB

Wheel lift

Low profile and versatile, with various grid options available so one platform covers passenger cars through light commercial recoveries.

DUAL LIGHTED PANEL

Hydraulics & controls

An easy to use, centralised side control panel — dual lighted for night work, so deck and lift are operated from one station without walking the vehicle.

IP68 ENCAPSULATED

Lighting

Sleek, low profile aerodynamic housings, fully encapsulated and IP68 rated for moisture, vibration and corrosion resistance.

MOULDED POLYPROPYLENE

Toolboxes

Custom moulded nylon polypropylene with high resistance to flammability, water and dust ingress — kept dry and serviceable in the field.
