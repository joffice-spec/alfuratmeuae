Overview

High-tensile steel. Hydraulic telescopic reach.

Hydrolift truck-mounted knuckle-boom cranes are built for commercial vehicle applications — delivery fleets, building-material handling and service operations. High-tensile steel construction and hydraulic telescopic booms support versatile material handling from a folded, chassis-mounted envelope. The range spans a compact 5.3 tm unit through to a heavy-duty family reaching 16 metres, all presented and supported through Al Furat.

  • Four models — 675 K2, 1175 K2, 1675 and the 2475 (K1–K6) family
  • Verified lifting data reproduced directly from the manufacturer brochures
  • Configuration-dependent safety and control packages: Hx, HPS, HPro and HPro+
  • Manufacturer cites EN 12999 for loader-crane design

Engineering

Anatomy of a knuckle-boom crane.

The knuckle (articulated) boom folds compactly behind the cab, then unfolds and telescopes out under hydraulic power. The callouts below identify the main assemblies common to the Hydrolift range — each supported by the manufacturer material.

1 2 3 4 5 6
  • 1

    Base & slewing column

    Mounts to the chassis sub-frame and rotates the crane. Slewing angle 400° across the range; slewing torque scales with model.

  • 2

    Lift (first-boom) cylinder

    The hydraulic ram that raises the main boom. Working pressure runs from 240 bar on the 675 to 300 bar on the 2475.

  • 3

    Knuckle joint

    The articulation between first and outer boom that lets the crane fold compactly and reach down below deck level.

  • 4

    Telescopic outer boom

    Hydraulically extending sections set the reach — and, on the 2475, define the K1–K6 configurations from 6 m to 16 m.

  • 5

    Boom tip & attachment point

    The lifting point at full extension, where rated capacity is quoted against radius on the load chart.

  • 6

    Hydraulic hose routing & high-tensile steel

    Protected hose runs feed the cylinders; booms are fabricated from high-tensile steel for strength at low weight.

Callouts identify assemblies described in the manufacturer material. They are positioned on a representative studio image and are not a scaled engineering drawing.

Stabiliser / outrigger spread

Stability comes from the outrigger (stabiliser) spread, quoted per model with a standard and an extra-long option. Capacity figures apply only at the approved support configuration.

  • 675 K2 — standard 3,130 mm · extra-long 4,000 mm
  • 1175 K2 — standard 3,900 mm · extra-long 4,800 mm
  • 2475 — standard 5,100 mm

Standard vs. configuration-dependent

The Hx control package is the standard baseline. Overload protection (HPS), radio remote (HPro) and the combined safety/remote/monitoring package (HPro+) are configuration-dependent and specified at order. Stabiliser spread, extension count (2475 K1–K6) and mounting are confirmed per installation.
