The machine

Built for fast, controlled vehicle removal

The AQUILA 100 combines a rear fork, versatile Z-arm and winch-assisted recovery system in a compact platform designed for everyday vehicle-removal work.

  1. 1Rear forkRemoves a parked vehicle by positioning itself perpendicular to the vehicle being removed.
  2. 2Z-armVersatile rear arm for operating in difficult or restricted situations.
  3. 3Fixed boomCarries the winch and the pulley guide at its end.
  4. 4Winch pulley guideRedirects the winch rope to assist approach and alignment.
  5. 5Hand-control systemEvery manoeuvre is performed from the hand control.

Capabilities

Rear fork, Z-arm and hand control

Designed for recovery from the start — not adapted for it.

Rear fork

Designed for difficult removal angles

The rear-fork device can position itself perpendicular to the vehicle being removed, helping operators work in constrained or challenging environments.

Schematic illustration of the described perpendicular positioning. Not to scale; angles indicative only.

Z-arm

Versatility where access is limited

The rear Z-arm gives operators the flexibility to work around difficult vehicle positions and restricted spaces.

  • Positions the fork for awkward approaches.
  • Works around obstacles in tight environments.
  • Retaining strap secures the load during the lift.
Hand control

Control every manoeuvre from the hand control

The operating system is designed to make recovery movements direct, controlled and easy to manage.

  • All manoeuvres are performed from the hand control.
  • The rear fork can remove a parked vehicle without the operator leaving the cab.
Winch & pulley guide

Winch guidance when alignment matters

The pulley guide at the end of the fixed boom redirects the winch rope to assist the approach and positioning of the vehicle being recovered.

Schematic of the rope path via the fixed-boom pulley guide.
